# Three reasons your houseplant is dying — ~40 s (Opus run)

Audience: first-time plant owners. Accent: orange. CTA: Comment PLANT.
Hook promise in the first 3 s: "it's not you — it's one of three things".

| Beat | Voice | On the desk |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Your houseplant isn't dying because you're bad with plants. It's dying for one of three reasons. | wilted plant already on the desk at frame 1; "not you." handwritten; "3 REASONS" heading pops + marker underline |
| 01 | One. You love it too much. Most houseplants die from too much water, not too little. Push a finger into the soil. Dry two knuckles deep? Water it. Still damp? Wait. | card 01 TOO MUCH LOVE; watering can slides in; photo of a finger in the soil; marker arrow to the knuckle; sticky note "dry → water / damp → wait" |
| 02 | Two. The wrong window. A north window gets a fraction of the light a south window does. Move the plant, not the couch. | card 02 THE WRONG WINDOW; two prints side by side (dim north / sunny south); slash over north, ring around south; the plant moves from north to south |
| 03 | Three. The pot. No drainage hole, and the roots sit in water until they rot. A pot with a hole costs three dollars. | card 03 THE POT; pot cut-out with its hole ringed; photo of rotten roots; "$3" tag |
| Close | Fix those three, and your plant will outlive your phone. | camera lifts to the whole desk |
| CTA | Comment PLANT, and I'll send you the one-page checklist. | checklist page types itself; COMMENT PLANT stamp; Claude cursor ticks it; paper plane |

## Sources
- UConn Home & Garden Education Center, "Ailing Houseplants" — improper watering is the number-one cause of houseplant death. https://homegarden.cahnr.uconn.edu/factsheets/ailing-houseplants/
- UConn, "Houseplant Watering Recommendations" — check soil moisture before watering; use pots with drainage holes. https://homegarden.cahnr.uconn.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/3479/2022/08/Houseplant-Watering-Recommendations.pdf
- Mississippi State University Extension P1012 — north windows often give under 200 foot-candles, south windows 500–1,000+ on the sill. https://extension.msstate.edu/sites/default/files/publications/publications/P1012_web-1.pdf
