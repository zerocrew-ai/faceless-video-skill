Your houseplant isn't dying because you're bad with plants. It's dying for one of three reasons.

One. You love it too much. Most houseplants die from too much water, not too little. Push a finger into the soil. Dry two knuckles deep? Water it. Still damp? Wait.

Two. The wrong window. A north window gets a fraction of the light a south window does. Move the plant, not the couch.

Three. The pot. No drainage hole, and the roots sit in water until they rot. A pot with a hole costs three dollars.
